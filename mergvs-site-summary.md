# PART A — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

## A.1 The company in one paragraph

MERGVS is a **spatial computing studio** (established 2026) that builds **bespoke, immersive, real-time 3D marketing experiences for luxury real estate**. For each exceptional property, MERGVS creates a dedicated cinematic microsite — a fully navigable 3D reconstruction of the home with spatial navigation, ambient sound, virtual staging, and architectural storytelling — that opens from **a single URL in any browser on phone, desktop, tablet, or VR headset, with no app, no account, and no download**. The company is headquartered across **Luxembourg, Milan, and Istanbul** and works worldwide. Its defining business innovation is a **commission-only model for estate agents**: MERGVS produces everything at its own cost and risk and is paid only when the property sells. Contact: **hello@mergvs.com**. The site is bilingual (EN/TR).

## A.2 The core idea

- **Problem:** Luxury homes are sold through emotion, but their digital presentation is "stuck in 2008" — static photos, flat grids, disconnected floor plans — while buying has gone fully online (100% of 2024 buyers searched online; 63% offered without visiting).
- **Solution:** Give every exceptional property "its own world" — a hand-coded, never-templated 3D experience built with Three.js/WebXR from the client's existing plans and photos (no site visit needed), reconstructing the architecture, light, and atmosphere so buyers anywhere feel present before they arrive.
- **Motto-level phrases:** "Every exceptional property deserves its own world." / "The listing *is* the experience." / "One link; the world opens."

## A.3 The two commercial models

1. **For estate agents — commission-only (flagship).** Zero upfront cost, no retainer, no subscription, no deposit, no exclusivity. MERGVS funds the entire production and invoices only at closing: **0.8%** of final sale price for properties **≥ €2M**, **1.2%** for properties **< €2M**. Everything is included (microsite, 3D tour, cinematic video, deployment, analytics, unlimited revisions until sale). One-page digital agreement within 24h; payment due 14 days after completion. If the listing is withdrawn or the agent cancels mid-production, **half the agreed commission** is due. The experience **remains MERGVS's IP** (licensed sales tool). MERGVS claims to be the only commission-only real estate media model in Europe where the studio bears the entire production risk.
2. **For private sellers — flat-rate.** No commission; the seller **owns the assets outright**. **Core** from €3,500 (3D environment, photography, floor plans, 10 working days); **Full Production** from €6,500 (adds cinematic video, virtual staging, branded microsite); **Bespoke** quoted individually (properties above €5M, landmark architecture). Proposal within 24 hours.

## A.4 The product in brief

A real-time 3D model of the property is the heart of every engagement. Layered around it: first-person walk-throughs, orbit views of the façade, cinematic 3D video rendered from the model (no drone/film crew), virtual staging with day/night lighting, interactive floor plans, material and finish galleries, neighbourhood/lifestyle mapping, presentation boards, photography retouching, one-click agent contact, and full buyer-engagement analytics (heatmaps, dwell time, funnels). Delivery: bespoke experience in ~7–10 days (standard agent engagement 10–15 working days). Four-step process: Discovery → 3D Reconstruction → Experience Build → Go Live.

## A.5 The people and identity in brief

Founded by two architects trained at Istanbul Bilgi University and **Politecnico di Milano** — **Yiğit Özen** (founder of the spatial-web studio *decentralize* in Milan and *Virtually Ever After* in Luxembourg) and **Deniz Ağaoğlu** (co-founder of *Virtually Ever After*, 2025). The studio is explicitly *not* an estate agency: it does architecture + code. The name comes from **Mergus**, the diving bird — same Latin root (*mergere*, to dive) as "immersive": MERGVS dives beneath the surface of a property to reveal its hidden story.

## A.6 Markets and audience in brief

Territories: **Luxembourg** (château estates, cross-border discretion), **Milan** (Brera penthouses, Navigli conversions), **Istanbul** (Bosphorus villas, Ottoman mansions); international work case-by-case. Audience: global HNWIs/UHNWIs and family offices — remote-transacting, experience-seeking luxury buyers — and the agents who serve them.

## A.7 Evidence base in brief

Every claim on the site is paired with a named third-party source: Texas Tech/Matterport (+9% sale price, 31% faster closing), Apartments.com (49% more qualified leads, 3–6× engagement), NAR (403% more enquiries with video), Redfin (63% remote offers), Knight Frank (PIRI 100, Wealth Report, Family Office Report), Savills (33% branded-residence premium), VHT Studios (photography quality gap), PhotoUp (5–10× dwell time).
