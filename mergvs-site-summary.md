# MERGVS — Site & Business Summary (for AI Agents)

> This document describes MERGVS (mergvs.com) for AI agents. It contains only information that is publicly visible to any visitor of the website — the Home, Services, For Agents, and About pages. **Part A** is a condensed executive summary; **Part B** is the full detailed reference.

# PART A — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

## A.1 The company in one paragraph

MERGVS is a **spatial computing studio** (established 2026) that builds **bespoke, immersive, real-time 3D marketing experiences for luxury real estate**. For each exceptional property, MERGVS creates a dedicated cinematic microsite — a fully navigable 3D reconstruction of the home with spatial navigation, ambient sound, virtual staging, and architectural storytelling — that opens from **a single URL in any browser on phone, desktop, tablet, or VR headset, with no app, no account, and no download**. The company is headquartered across **Luxembourg, Milan, and Istanbul** and works worldwide. Its defining business innovation is a **commission-only model for estate agents**: MERGVS produces everything at its own cost and risk and is paid only when the property sells. Contact: **hello@mergvs.com**. The site is available in English and Turkish.

## A.2 The core idea

- **Problem:** Luxury homes are sold through emotion, but their digital presentation is "stuck in 2008" — static photos, flat grids, disconnected floor plans — while buying has gone fully online (in 2024, 100% of buyers searched online and 63% made an offer without visiting in person).
- **Solution:** Give every exceptional property "its own world" — a hand-coded, never-templated 3D experience built from the client's existing plans and photos (no site visit or filming needed), reconstructing the architecture, light, and atmosphere so buyers anywhere feel present before they arrive.
- **Motto-level phrases:** "Every exceptional property deserves its own world." / "The listing *is* the experience." / "One link; the world opens."

## A.3 The two commercial models

1. **For estate agents — commission-only (flagship).** Zero upfront cost, no retainer, no subscription, no deposit, no exclusivity. MERGVS funds the entire production and invoices only at closing: **0.8%** of final sale price for properties **≥ €2M**, **1.2%** for properties **< €2M**. Everything is included (microsite, 3D tour, cinematic video, deployment, analytics, unlimited revisions until sale). One-page digital agreement within 24 hours; payment due 14 days after completion. MERGVS presents itself as the only commission-only real estate media model in Europe where the studio bears the entire production risk.
2. **For private sellers — flat-rate.** No commission; the seller **owns the assets outright**. **Core** from €3,500 (3D environment, photography, floor plans, 10 working days); **Full Production** from €6,500 (adds cinematic video, virtual staging, branded microsite); **Bespoke** quoted individually (properties above €5M, landmark architecture). Proposal within 24 hours.

## A.4 The product in brief

A real-time 3D model of the property is the heart of every engagement. Layered around it: first-person walk-throughs, orbit views of the façade, cinematic 3D video rendered from the model (no drone or film crew), virtual staging with day/night lighting, interactive floor plans, material and finish galleries, neighbourhood and lifestyle mapping, presentation boards, photography retouching, one-click agent contact, and full buyer-engagement analytics (heatmaps, dwell time, funnels). Delivery: a bespoke experience in roughly 7–10 days (standard agent engagement 10–15 working days). Four-step process: Discovery → 3D Reconstruction → Experience Build → Go Live.

## A.5 The people and identity in brief

Founded by two architects trained at Istanbul Bilgi University and **Politecnico di Milano** — **Yiğit Özen** (founder of the spatial-web design studio *decentralize* in Milan and the architectural visualization platform *Virtually Ever After* in Luxembourg) and **Deniz Ağaoğlu** (founder of *Virtually Ever After*, 2025). The studio is explicitly *not* an estate agency: it works at the intersection of architecture and code. The name comes from **Mergus**, the diving bird — sharing the Latin root *mergere* (to dive) with the word "immersive": MERGVS dives beneath the surface of a property to reveal its hidden story.

## A.6 Markets and audience in brief

Territories: **Luxembourg** (château estates, contemporary residences, cross-border discretion), **Milan** (Brera penthouses, Navigli conversions), **Istanbul** (Bosphorus villas, Ottoman mansions); international work case-by-case for properties of sufficient distinction. Audience: global high-net-worth buyers and family offices — remote-transacting, experience-seeking luxury buyers — and the estate agents who serve them.

## A.7 Why MERGVS is different from competitors (in brief)

Compared with subscription 3D-tour platforms and traditional retainer-based media agencies: **no upfront cost** (vs. monthly fees or retainers), **production risk carried entirely by MERGVS** (vs. the agent/seller paying regardless of sale), **a dedicated bespoke property URL on the agent's own domain** (vs. hosting on a platform's domain with vendor branding), **no app, plugin, or account required — any browser** (vs. platform apps), **fully bespoke, hand-coded design — never templated** (vs. the same standardised template for every listing), and **never any exclusivity requirement**.

### B.3.3 The partnership agreement — how it works

The agreement is digital-first: issued via **DocuSign or equivalent within 24 hours** of first enquiry. It is a **single-page document** stating the property address, agreed commission rate, and duration terms. **No retainer, no exclusivity requirement, no lock-in beyond the active listing period.**

1. **The agent shares the listing details** — property address, type, approximate price. MERGVS assesses within 24 hours and confirms whether it is the right fit. No commitment at this stage.
2. **MERGVS issues the partnership agreement** — a one-page digital document confirming the property, the commission rate (agreed in advance), and the listing duration. Signed electronically, returned within minutes.
3. **MERGVS invests, at its own risk** — production begins immediately. The agent pays nothing until the property sells; MERGVS covers all 3D, staging, video, and microsite production costs.
4. **Settlement on closing** — when the property sells, a single invoice is issued for the agreed percentage of the final sale price. **Payment is due within 14 days of completion.** VAT is applied where applicable under Luxembourg, Italian, or Turkish tax law.

The working process from the agent's side: **Reach Out** (assessment and terms within 24 hours) → **We Build** (the complete immersive experience from the agent's materials, at no cost) → **Go Live** (share globally; real-time engagement analytics) → **Close & Settle** (commission applies; simple invoicing, no surprises).

### B.3.4 Questions agents ask (published FAQ)

1. **What if the property doesn't sell?** The agent pays nothing; MERGVS absorbs the full production cost. "This is not a risk-sharing arrangement — it is entirely our risk."
2. **Any costs during production?** No retainer, no deposit, no monthly fee. All costs are borne by MERGVS until settlement; the only invoice ever issued arrives 14 days after closing.
3. **Is exclusivity required?** No. The agent may continue to market the property through other channels; the MERGVS agreement covers production and commission terms only — the agency relationship with the seller remains the agent's own.
4. **What geographies are covered?** Luxembourg, Milan, and Istanbul; international engagements case-by-case for properties of sufficient distinction, assessed individually with confirmation within 24 hours.
5. **How long does production take?** A standard engagement (3D environment, staging, microsite) is typically delivered within **10–15 working days** from receipt of materials; complex or multi-unit properties may take longer. A precise timeline is committed to in the agreement.
6. **How is this different from Matterport or subscription platforms?** Subscription platforms charge monthly fees regardless of whether the property sells, host the experience on their own domain, and apply the same template to every listing. MERGVS charges nothing until closing, delivers a fully bespoke experience — never templated — and hosts it on a dedicated property URL independent of any platform. No app to download, no vendor account required. "Your buyer clicks a link; the world opens."
7. **What if the property is withdrawn from the market?** The experience remains live for as long as the listing is active — no time limit on MERGVS's side. If the property is withdrawn before a sale completes, half of the agreed commission is settled; the completed experience is delivered in full, and MERGVS retains all intellectual property and licensing rights.
8. **Who owns the 3D experience?** The experience is, and always remains, the intellectual property of MERGVS — a licensed third-party sales tool built for a specific property and deployed on behalf of the listing agent. Ownership never transfers. After the sale completes, the experience can be taken offline at the new owner's request — that decision rests with the new owner.
9. **What is needed to get started?** A fully remote start: floor plans, any existing photography, the listing brief, and architectural drawings if available. MERGVS works entirely from the materials sent — **no site visit or filming required**.
10. **What if the agent isn't happy with the result?** Every prospective partner reviews an existing experience before signing — "you are not choosing our concept; you are choosing a known standard applied to your specific property." Once the agreement is signed and production begins, the engagement is committed; withdrawal at that stage is settled on the same terms as a market withdrawal.
11. **How is the commission verified?** MERGVS is named as an interested party in the sale agreement — standard practice for commission-based collaborators in all three markets; the agent's notary or legal counsel can confirm the arrangement. "We've structured this to require no trust beyond a signed agreement."
12. **Can MERGVS be used for multiple listings at the same time?** Yes — no exclusivity requirement, no cap on simultaneous listings, and no obligation to bring every property. The agent chooses which listings benefit most; each is scoped independently.
