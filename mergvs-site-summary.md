## 1. What MERGVS Is (One-Paragraph Definition)

MERGVS is a **spatial computing studio** (founded/established 2026) that builds **bespoke, immersive, real-time 3D marketing experiences for luxury real estate**. For each exceptional property, MERGVS creates a dedicated cinematic microsite — a fully navigable 3D reconstruction of the home with spatial navigation, ambient sound, virtual staging, and architectural storytelling — that opens from **a single URL in any browser on phone, desktop, tablet, or VR headset, with no app, no account, and no download**. The company is headquartered across three cities — **Luxembourg, Milan, and Istanbul** — and works worldwide. Its defining business innovation is a **commission-only model for estate agents**: MERGVS produces everything at its own cost and risk, and is paid only when the property sells. Contact: **hello@mergvs.com**. The site is bilingual (EN / TR).

**Brand essence / tagline:** "Every exceptional property deserves its own world." The listing *is* the experience — MERGVS turns a listing into a destination.

## 3. The Product / Technology (Home + Services pages)

**Core principle:** Everything MERGVS builds starts from one thing — a **real-time 3D model of the property**, reconstructed from the client's plans, drawings, photography, and references (no site visit or filming required). Around that model, MERGVS layers staging, light, floor plans, video, and a bespoke microsite.

**Technology stack / approach:**
- **Three.js and WebXR** — hand-coded, real-time 3D on the open web (the "spatial web").
- **Single codebase, every device**: one URL runs on mobile, desktop, tablet, and VR headsets. No app, no plugin, no platform account.
- **No templates, ever.** Every experience is custom-designed and built from scratch for the specific property.
- Hosted on a **dedicated bespoke property URL** (can be on the agent's domain, not a vendor platform's domain; no vendor branding).

**Experience features (what a buyer gets):**
- Interactive floor plan with room-by-room navigation
- First-person walk-through with teleport between rooms; feel of proportions
- Orbit 3D of exterior/façade, massing, and approach; comparable façade options
- Cinematic 3D video sequences rendered directly from the model (fly-throughs, reveals — no drone, no film crew, no scheduling), with spatial audio design
- Virtual staging / live furnishing: place furniture in real time, load ready-made sets, style alternatives, day & night views
- Ambient lighting simulation for different times of day (golden hour, twilight, seasonal atmosphere)
- Curated material & finish close-up gallery (textures, swatches, comparisons)
- Neighbourhood context, views, proximity/lifestyle/transit mapping ("we sell the life, not just the walls")
- Presentation boards: digital architectural sheets — plans, sections, elevations, material palettes
- One-click direct contact to the listing agent
- **Analytics & engagement tracking**: buyer heatmaps, dwell time, interaction funnels, engagement scoring

**Production process (4 steps, from Home page):**
1. **Discovery** — learn the property's story: architecture, provenance, atmosphere, target buyer.
2. **3D Reconstruction** — rebuild the property in real-time 3D from plans, drawings, references.
3. **Experience Build** — designers and developers craft the bespoke microsite: spatial, cinematic, conversion-optimised.
4. **Go Live** — published, shared globally, tracked with full engagement analytics.

**Delivery time:** a bespoke experience in **7–10 days** (Home page); standard agent engagement typically **10–15 working days** once materials are received (Agents FAQ); Core private-seller package delivered within 10 working days.

## 4. Business Model & Pricing

MERGVS has **two commercial tracks**:

### 4.1 For Estate Agents — Commission-Only (the flagship model, `agents.html`)

**Positioning:** "We don't charge upfront. We earn together." MERGVS claims to be **the only commission-only real estate media model in Europe** where the production studio bears the entire production cost until the property sells. No retainer, no subscription, no deposit, no monthly fee, no exclusivity, no risk to the agent.

**Commission structure (percentage of final sale price, invoiced only at closing):**
- **Premium Properties** (valued €2,000,000 and above): **0.8%** — e.g. on a €3M sale → €24,000.
- **Standard Properties** (valued under €2,000,000): **1.2%** — e.g. on a €1.2M sale → €14,400.
- Both tiers include: full immersive microsite, 3D spatial tour with navigation, cinematic video production, cross-platform deployment, analytics & engagement tracking, and **unlimited revisions until sale**. No add-ons, no surprise invoices.

**Partnership agreement mechanics:**
- Issued **digitally (DocuSign or equivalent) within 24 hours** of first enquiry; a **single-page document** stating property address, agreed commission rate, and duration terms.
- **Four-step flow:** (1) Agent shares listing details (address, type, approximate price) — MERGVS assesses within 24 hours, no commitment; (2) MERGVS issues the one-page agreement, signed electronically; (3) MERGVS invests at its own risk — production begins immediately, agent pays nothing; (4) Settlement on closing — a single invoice for the agreed percentage, **payment due within 14 days of completion**; VAT applied where applicable under Luxembourg, Italian, or Turkish tax law.

**Key contractual terms (from the Agents FAQ):**
- **If the property doesn't sell:** the agent pays nothing; MERGVS absorbs the full production cost (entirely MERGVS's risk).
- **No exclusivity:** the agent may market through other channels; no cap on simultaneous listings; no obligation to bring every property.
- **Geographies:** Luxembourg, Milan, Istanbul; international engagements case-by-case for properties of sufficient distinction (assessed within 24 hours).
- **Withdrawal clause:** if the property is withdrawn from the market before sale (or the agent cancels after production begins), **half of the agreed commission becomes due**, and the completed experience is delivered in full.
- **No cancellation right after signing:** every prospective partner reviews an existing experience *before* signing — "you are choosing a known standard, not a concept."
- **IP ownership:** the 3D experience **always remains the intellectual property of MERGVS**; it functions as a licensed third-party sales tool. Ownership never transfers. After the sale, the *new owner* of the property may request it be taken offline.
- **Commission verification:** MERGVS is named as an **interested party in the sale agreement** — standard practice for commission-based collaborators in all three markets; verifiable by the notary/legal counsel.
- **Inputs needed to start:** existing materials sent remotely — floor plans, photography, listing brief, architectural drawings if available. No site visit or filming required.

**Competitive comparison (MERGVS vs. subscription platforms vs. traditional retainer):**
| Dimension | MERGVS | Subscription platforms (e.g. Matterport-style) | Traditional retainer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upfront cost | None | Monthly fee + setup | Retainer or day rate |
| Production risk | Borne entirely by MERGVS | Agent/seller pays regardless | Agent/seller pays regardless |
| Property URL | Dedicated bespoke domain | Hosted on platform's domain | Varies |
| App/account required | No — any browser | Often app or plugin | Varies |
| Experience design | Fully bespoke, never templated | Standardised template | Varies by budget |
| Exclusivity required | Never | N/A | Often yes |

### 4.2 For Private Sellers — Flat-Rate (from `services.html`)

If the seller has no agent, MERGVS offers flat-rate engagements — everything produced upfront, **no commission at closing**, and (unlike the agent model) **the seller owns the assets outright**. Every engagement is scoped individually; a precise proposal is produced within 24 hours of contact.

- **Core Package — from €3,500**: 3D interactive environment, architectural photography, floor plans. Delivered within 10 working days.
- **Full Production — from €6,500**: everything in Core plus cinematic 3D video, virtual staging, and branded microsite.
- **Bespoke Engagement — quoted individually**: for properties above €5M, landmark architecture, or estates requiring custom creative treatment.
