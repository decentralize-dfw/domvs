05 What agents ask

The difficult questions,
answered first.

01What happens if the property does not sell?

You pay nothing. We absorb the entire production cost. The risk sits with us, not with you and not with your seller.

02Are there any costs while you are building it?

None. No retainer, no deposit, no monthly fee. We carry every cost until the sale closes, and the only invoice you will ever see arrives fourteen days after completion.

03Do I have to give you exclusivity?

No. Keep marketing the property through any channel you like. Our agreement covers the production and the commission and nothing else. Your relationship with the seller stays entirely yours.

04Which countries do you work in?

Luxembourg, Italy and Türkiye, from offices in Luxembourg City, Milan and Istanbul. We take work elsewhere case by case and answer within 24 hours.

05How long does production take?

Ten to fifteen working days from the moment we have your material for a standard property: 3D environment, staging and the property website. Multi-unit or listed buildings take longer, and the date goes in the agreement.

06How is this different from Matterport or a subscription platform?

Subscription platforms bill you every month whether or not the property sells, host the tour on their domain under their brand, and apply one template to every listing. We charge nothing until closing, design the page around the specific property and publish it on your domain. Your buyer clicks a link and the house opens.

07What if the property is withdrawn from the market?

The experience stays live for as long as the listing is active, with no time limit on our side. If the property is withdrawn before a sale completes, half the agreed commission is settled, you receive the finished experience in full, and we keep the intellectual property and licensing rights.

08Who owns the 3D experience?

We do. It is a licensed sales tool built for one property and published on your behalf, and ownership does not transfer. Once the sale completes the new owner can ask us to take it offline, and that decision is theirs.

09What do you need from me to start?

Floor plans, any photographs that exist, the listing brief and architectural drawings if you have them. We work entirely from what you send. No site visit, no filming.

10What if I do not like the result?

Every agent reviews a finished experience before signing, so you know the standard you are buying. Once the agreement is signed and production starts the work is committed, and withdrawing at that point settles on the same terms as a market withdrawal.

11How do you make sure you get paid?

We are named as an interested party in the sale agreement, which is ordinary practice for commission-based collaborators in all three markets. Your notary or legal counsel can confirm the arrangement. Nothing here requires trust beyond a signed document.

12Can I use MERGVS on several listings at once?

Yes. There is no exclusivity, no cap and no obligation to bring us every property. Pick the listings where it will make a difference. Each one is scoped separately.
