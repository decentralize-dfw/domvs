05 — Questions agents ask

The awkward ones,
answered first.

01What if the property doesn’t sell?

You pay nothing. We absorb the full production cost. This is not a risk-sharing arrangement — it is entirely our risk.

02Are there any costs during production?

No retainer, no deposit, no monthly fee. We carry every cost until settlement. The only invoice you will ever see arrives 14 days after closing.

03Is exclusivity required?

No. Keep marketing the property through any channel you like. Our agreement covers production and commission terms only — your relationship with the seller stays yours.

04Which geographies do you cover?

Luxembourg, Milan and Istanbul. International engagements case-by-case for properties of sufficient distinction, assessed individually with confirmation within 24 hours.

05How long does production take?

A standard engagement — 3D environment, staging, microsite — is typically delivered within 10–15 working days from receipt of materials. Complex or multi-unit properties take longer; the exact timeline is committed to in the agreement.

06How is this different from Matterport or a subscription platform?

Subscription platforms charge monthly whether or not the property sells, host the experience on their own domain, and apply the same template to every listing. We charge nothing until closing, build a fully bespoke experience, and host it on a dedicated property URL independent of any platform. No app, no vendor account. Your buyer clicks a link; the world opens.

07What if the property is withdrawn from the market?

The experience stays live as long as the listing is active — no time limit on our side. If the property is withdrawn before a sale completes, half of the agreed commission is settled, the completed experience is delivered in full, and we retain all intellectual property and licensing rights.

08Who owns the 3D experience?

It is, and remains, the intellectual property of MERGVS — a licensed third-party sales tool built for a specific property and deployed on your behalf. Ownership never transfers. After completion the experience can be taken offline at the new owner’s request; that decision rests with them.

09What do you need to get started?

Floor plans, any existing photography, the listing brief, and architectural drawings if you have them. We work entirely from the materials you send — no site visit, no filming.

10What if I’m not happy with the result?

Every prospective partner reviews an existing experience before signing. You are not choosing our concept; you are choosing a known standard applied to your property. Once the agreement is signed and production begins the engagement is committed, and withdrawal at that stage is settled on the same terms as a market withdrawal.

11How is the commission verified?

We are named as an interested party in the sale agreement — standard practice for commission-based collaborators in all three markets. Your notary or legal counsel can confirm the arrangement. We have structured this to require no trust beyond a signed agreement.

12Can I use MERGVS for several listings at once?

Yes. No exclusivity, no cap on simultaneous listings, and no obligation to bring every property. You choose which listings benefit most; each is scoped independently.
