06 What agents ask

The difficult questions,
answered first.

01Do agents pay a different price?

No. Agents choose between three arrangements: a fixed production fee (A), a hybrid model (B), or commission only (C). The same three options are offered to every agent, and the terms are set out in the written quote for each mandate.

02When do I pay?

Under option A, 50% on signature and 50% on go-live, net fourteen days. Under option B, €5,000 on signature and the balance on completion. Under option C, nothing upfront; the fee is invoiced within 14 days of completion. No monthly fee, no retainer, no exclusivity.

03Is there anything tied to the sale?

Under options B and C, part or all of the fee is tied to completion, as set out in the written quote for the mandate. Option A is a fixed production fee with nothing tied to the sale.

04What if the seller withdraws after production has started?

If the property is withdrawn, or the agreement is cancelled after production starts, a flat fee of €8,000 (option C) is due. Under options A and B, amounts already paid are retained and nothing further is due. The completed experience is delivered in full.

05Do I have to give you exclusivity?

No. Keep marketing the property through any channel you like. The agreement covers the production and nothing else. Your relationship with the seller stays entirely yours.

06How long does it take?

Typically four to six weeks from the day plans and photographs arrive; the exact date is committed on the quote.

07Do you need to visit the property?

We work from plans and photographs. A visit is sometimes useful on a difficult house, and when it is, it goes in the quote.

08Which countries do you work in?

Luxembourg, Italy and Türkiye, from Luxembourg City, Milan and Istanbul. We take work elsewhere case by case and answer within 24 hours.

09How is this different from Matterport or a subscription platform?

A scan records a room that already exists and lives on their domain under their brand, for a monthly fee. We rebuild the house as a model, so the lighting, the staging and the video all come out of it, and the page sits on your domain.

10Who owns the finished experience?

The experience remains the intellectual property of MERGVS and is licensed for the marketing of this property. After completion, it is taken offline within 30 days unless the new owner requests a licence to keep it online.

11What do you need from me to start?

Floor plans, any photographs that exist, the address and the living area. The itemised quote follows within 24 hours.

12Can I use MERGVS on several listings at once?

Yes. There is no cap and no obligation. Pick the listings where it will make a difference. Each one is scoped and quoted separately.
