04 Common questions

What people ask
before they send a plan.

01What is a 3D virtual property tour, exactly?

A 3D reconstruction of the property that opens in a web browser. The buyer moves through the rooms, looks out of the windows, reads the floor plan and sees the finishes, all from one link. It is not a set of panoramic photographs stitched together. The house exists as geometry, which is why the lighting, the video and the staging can all change.

02Do you need to visit the property or film it?

No. We work from floor plans, architectural drawings and whatever photographs already exist. That is the whole input. It also means we can start on a property that is occupied, unbuilt or on another continent.

03How is this different from Matterport?

Matterport scans a room that exists and hosts the result on its own domain with its own branding, on a monthly fee. We rebuild the property as a 3D model, design the page around that specific house, and publish it on your domain. Because it is geometry rather than a scan we can restage it, relight it and render video from it.

04Does the buyer need an app or a headset?

Neither. It opens in Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a phone, a tablet or a laptop. If the buyer happens to own a headset it works there too, through WebXR.

05How long does it take?

Seven to ten working days for a finished experience once we have your material. Larger or multi-unit properties take longer, and we commit to a date before we start.

06What does it cost?

For estate agents, nothing upfront. We are paid 0.8% of the final sale price on properties at or above €2,000,000 and 1.2% below that, invoiced when the sale closes. For private sellers, from €3,500 flat.
