four trajectories.

02Navigli and Porta Genova The canal district, rebuilt by the design community. Industrial loft conversions and warehouse residences from the early 1900s. ·

03 Common questions

What agents here
ask first.

01Do you work with agencies in Milan?

Yes. We work across Brera, Navigli, Isola, Porta Nuova, the Centro Storico and the Quadrilatero, on the same commission-only terms we use everywhere else.

02Can you show an unrenovated apartment as it would look finished?

Yes, and in Milan this is most of the value. We model the shell as it stands and then stage it digitally, so the buyer sees both the apartment today and the apartment after the work, from the same link.

03Do you produce in Italian?

Yes. Pages ship in Italian and English together, which is how most Milan prime buyers actually read a listing.

04Do you need to visit the property?

No. We work from floor plans, drawings and existing photographs. Nothing needs to be scheduled and nobody has to be at the house.

05What does it cost an agent?

Nothing upfront. We are paid 0.8% of the final sale price on properties at or above €2,000,000 and 1.2% below that, invoiced when the sale closes.
