01 Where we came from

Trained in architecture.
Working in code.

MERGVS is what Virtually Ever After does for property: the same studio, pointed at houses instead of exhibitions and virtual worlds. Both founders studied architecture at the Politecnico di Milano. That is where the proportion, the material sense and the habit of drawing before building come from. The other half of the studio is software: Three.js, WebXR and the browser graphics stack, written once and running on a phone, a laptop and a headset without a separate build for each.

02 The name

Mergus,
the bird that dives.

Everything worth knowing about a house is below the surface of the photograph.
How we work

MERGVS comes from Mergus, the Latin name of the diving duck. The word "immersive" shares its root, mergere, meaning to dip or to sink. The name was chosen because the job is to go past the elevation and the estate agent photograph and come back with the thing that actually sells the house.
