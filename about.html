02 — The name

Mergus.
The bird that dives.

We don’t decorate the surface — we dive beneath it, into the architecture, the light, the atmosphere, and bring back the hidden story.
— The MERGVS philosophy

MERGVS comes from Mergus, the diving bird that plunges past the reflections to find what others cannot see. The word “immersive” shares the same Latin root — mergere, to dive, to submerge. The meaning is already there, waiting inside every house. We simply reveal it.
