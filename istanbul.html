01 — The market

Ottoman heritage,
continental scarcity.

Roughly twenty-nine kilometres of navigable coastline, strict height restrictions protecting the skyline, and a heritage designation system that prevents demolition of significant structures. Together they create supply constraints with no equivalent in Western Europe.

MERGVS brings immersive spatial computing to that market — where Ottoman-era yalı mansions, modern Bosphorus-view apartments and historic Beyoğlu buildings all need a presentation that captures the spatial drama photography cannot convey.
