01 The market

A market that runs
on discretion.

Luxembourg does not behave like Paris, London or Geneva. Demand comes from fund managers, EU institutional staff and a cross-border executive class, and those buyers put privacy above exposure. A large share of the best properties never appear on a portal at all.

That is the case for a 3D tour rather than a public listing. One link goes to a named buyer, shows the whole house, and tracks what they looked at. The property gets a proper viewing without becoming public information.

03 Common questions

What agents here
ask first.

01Do you work with agents in Luxembourg City?

Yes. Luxembourg is where the studio is based, and we work with agents across the Grand Duchy: Kirchberg, Limpertsberg, Belair, Cents, Hamm, Cloche d'Or and the château estates outside the city.

02Can you keep a property off the public portals?

Yes, and most of our Luxembourg work is handled that way. The property gets its own URL that you send to named buyers. It is not indexed, not listed and not discoverable, and you can see exactly who opened it.

03Which languages do you deliver in?

English, French, German and Turkish. The page can carry more than one, which matters for a market where the buyer, the seller and the notary rarely share one first language.

04Do you need to visit the property?

No. We work from floor plans, drawings and existing photographs. Nothing needs to be scheduled and nobody has to be at the house.

05What does it cost an agent?

Nothing upfront. We are paid 0.8% of the final sale price on properties at or above €2,000,000 and 1.2% below that, invoiced when the sale closes.
