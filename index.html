03 — The model

We carry the production risk.

For estate agents · commission only

Nothing to pay until
the property sells.
  • 0.8% of final sale price — properties at or above €2M
  • 1.2% of final sale price — properties below €2M
  • €0 upfront. No retainer, no subscription, no exclusivity
  • 14d invoiced at closing, payable within fourteen days

For private sellers · flat rate

Or buy the work
and own it outright.
  • €3.5k Core — 3D environment, photography, floor plans
  • €6.5k Full Production — adds cinematic video, staging, microsite
  • POA Bespoke — landmark architecture above €5M

Production is funded entirely by the studio: we build, deploy and revise at our own cost and risk, and are paid when the property sells. To our knowledge it is the only commission-only property media model in Europe. Delivery runs 7–10 days to a live experience.
